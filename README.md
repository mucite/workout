# Recomp · Phase 1

A personal training and nutrition plan for body recomposition: **70 → 75 kg**, building muscle while staying lean.
One page, no build step, works offline on your phone. Progress is saved in your browser.

---

## The week

Three full-body sessions, each about 45–55 min including warm-up and cool-down. Every session trains legs, chest, back, arms and core.

| Day | Session | Exercises |
|-----|---------|-----------|
| Mon | **Heavy** · strength, 5–8 reps | Back squat · Bench press · Assisted pull-ups · Hammer curls · Hanging leg raises |
| Tue | Rest | Walk 8–10k steps |
| Wed | **Volume** · muscle, 8–12 reps | Romanian deadlifts · Incline dumbbell press · Lat pulldowns · Triceps pushdowns · Cable crunches |
| Thu | Rest | Walk 8–10k steps |
| Fri | **Endurance** · higher reps, short rests | Bulgarian split squats · Push-ups · Pull-ups to max reps · Seated dumbbell press · Farmer's walks |
| Sat | **Zone 2** | 40 min easy cardio on a bike, rower or incline treadmill |
| Sun | Rest | Recover |

**Why it works**
- **Frequency:** every major muscle is trained at least twice a week, which beats once a week for growth.
- **Volume:** about 10–12 hard sets per muscle per week, the range where most lifters gain well.
- **Rep ranges:** heavy, moderate and high reps across the week build strength, size and muscular endurance together.
- **Rest:** 2 min on heavy compound lifts and 60–90 s on accessories. Longer rest on big lifts preserves performance.
- **Effort:** stop each set 1–2 reps short of failure. When you hit the top of the rep range on every set, add weight next session.
- **Zone 2** means an easy, steady pace at about 60–70 % of max heart rate (max HR ≈ 220 − age). You can breathe through your nose and talk in full sentences. It builds your aerobic base without hurting recovery.

**Warm-up and cool-down** are written for each day. The warm-up prepares that day's main lifts: one movement per step, each drill with a short demo video, then ramp-up sets. The cool-down stretches the muscles just trained.

## Nutrition

Whole foods, **Bio (EU organic)** where possible, bought fresh: nothing frozen, canned or ready-made. No sweeteners or "light" products. The only supplement is one whey smoothie after training.

Three training menus (Mon, Wed, Fri) and two rest-day menus that alternate (Tue + Sat, Thu + Sun), so meals change through the week while the macros stay the same.

| | kcal | Protein | Carbs | Fat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Training day | ~2,360 | ~159 g | ~270 g | ~68 g |
| Rest day | ~2,230 | ~158 g | ~215 g | ~79 g |

Weigh yourself once a week and aim for about +0.25 kg/week. If you stall for 2–3 weeks, add ~150 kcal.

## In the app

- **Train:** warm-up, workout and cool-down checklists. Tap a set to start the rest timer. Log your weights. Each exercise has a short form video and a machine alternative, folded away until you need them.
- **Eat:** the menu for the selected day, picked automatically, with per-meal macros. Tick meals off as you eat them.
- **Shop:** one shopping list covering every menu in the week, grouped by aisle.
