# Recomp · Phase 1

A personal training and nutrition plan for body recomposition: **70 → 75 kg**, building muscle while staying lean.
One page, no build step, works offline on your phone. Progress is saved in your browser.

---

## The week

| Day | Session | Focus |
|-----|---------|-------|
| Mon | **Upper** | Bench press · Assisted pull-ups · Seated dumbbell press · Hammer curls · Triceps pushdowns |
| Tue | Rest | Walk 8–10k steps |
| Wed | **Lower** | Leg press · Romanian deadlifts · Hamstring curls · Hanging leg raises |
| Thu | Rest | Walk 8–10k steps |
| Fri | **Full body** | Incline dumbbell press · Lat pulldowns · Bulgarian split squats · Farmer's walks · Plank |
| Sat | **Zone 2** | 30–45 min easy cardio (bike, incline walk or rower) |
| Sun | Rest | Recover |

**Why this split:** three hard sessions hit chest, back, legs and core twice a week while leaving 48 h between lifting days. One Zone 2 session builds your aerobic base without eating into recovery.

**Zone 2** means an easy, steady pace at about 60–70 % of max heart rate (max HR ≈ 220 − age). You can breathe through your nose and talk in full sentences. If you can't, slow down.

**Progression:** when you hit the top of the rep range on every set, add weight next session. Lower every rep slowly, over about 3 seconds.

## Nutrition

Whole foods, **Bio (EU organic)** where possible, bought fresh: nothing frozen, canned or ready-made. No protein powder, sweeteners or "light" products.

| | kcal | Protein | Carbs | Fat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Training day (Mon, Wed, Fri) | ~2,285 | 157 g | 264 g | 64 g |
| Rest day (other days) | ~2,230 | 156 g | 217 g | 78 g |

- **Training days:** oats & Skyr · chicken, rice & broccoli · banana + sourdough before the gym · beef & potatoes · quark at night
- **Rest days:** eggs on sourdough · turkey & lentil salad · Skyr & apple · salmon & sweet potato · kefir & pear
- Weigh yourself once a week and aim for about +0.25 kg/week. If you stall for 2–3 weeks, add ~150 kcal (extra rice, potatoes or a handful of nuts).

## In the app

- **Train:** warm-up, workout and cool-down checklists. Tap a set to start the rest timer. Log your weights. Form videos and machine alternatives are folded under each exercise.
- **Eat:** daily menu with per-meal macros. Tick meals off as you eat them.
- **Shop:** weekly shopping list grouped by aisle. Buy meat and fish in two runs (Mon + Thu) so they stay fresh.
